RESEARCH INTERESTS

Mechanisms, metabolism and human health.

I am interested in how molecular and cellular mechanisms respond to nutrients and metabolic state, and how those changes scale toward physiology, disease risk and human health.

  • Nutrient-responsive signaling and metabolic regulation
  • Nutrigenomics and gene–diet interactions
  • Enzyme activity, metabolic pathways and biomarker development
  • Lipid and membrane dynamics in nutrient sensing
  • Molecular determinants of diet-related disease
  • Cellular energy homeostasis and metabolic adaptation
