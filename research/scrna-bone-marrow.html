SINGLE-CELL RNA-SEQ ANALYSIS OF BONE MARROW

A computational project using R, SoupX and Seurat to process bone-marrow single-cell RNA-seq data from raw count correction through clustering and cell-type annotation.

Approach

The analysis used SoupX to account for ambient RNA contamination, followed by Seurat-based preprocessing, neighborhood graph construction, clustering and UMAP visualization. Cluster identities were then interpreted using marker information.
